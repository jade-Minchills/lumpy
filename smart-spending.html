Your step-by-step guide

The full framework, one page.

The Smart Spending Framework replaces emotion-driven budgeting with a tiered decision system. Your spending tier for any month is determined by your income — not by how you feel about money.

The infographic covers all four steps: sorting your expenses into tiers, reviewing your spending profile, simulating different income months, and generating your personal four-rule spending system.

Three tiers. Four rules. Zero guilt. The system works the same every month, whether you earned R20,000 or R80,000.

The three tiers

What tier are you in this month?

Every expense belongs to a tier. Every tier has a rule. The tier you spend in is determined by the income you receive — not by how you feel.

Tier 1
Must Pay

Non-negotiable commitments paid in full every month, regardless of income. Missing these has serious consequences.

  • Rent or bond repayment
  • Medical aid premium
  • Vehicle finance
  • Loan repayments
  • Essential insurance
  • Data and connectivity
Tier 2
Important

Meaningful for quality of life and paid in full in normal months. Can be reviewed in genuinely lean months.

  • Groceries above basic level
  • Domestic help
  • Gym membership
  • School activity fees
  • Household maintenance
  • Garden services
Tier 3
Flexible

Fully discretionary. Enjoyed in good months and paused without guilt in lean months. Suspending Tier 3 is the system working.

  • Dining out and takeaways
  • Travel and experiences
  • Clothing and accessories
  • Entertainment
  • Streaming services
  • Hobbies and personal care

Ask one question for each expense: "What genuinely happens if I skip this for one month?" Serious consequences = Tier 1. You'd notice and be uncomfortable = Tier 2. You'd be fine = Tier 3.

The month simulator

Know your spending plan before the month starts.

On the 25th of every month, open the simulator, drag the slider to your estimated income, and note which tiers are active. That is your spending plan for the coming month — set in two minutes.

As the slider moves, the signal updates automatically: a plain-English instruction, a bar chart showing allocation across all tiers, and exact rand amounts for each category.

Try dragging it to zero. Your system is working if Tier 1 is still paid from The Shield and you feel no panic — because The Shield exists for exactly this scenario.
