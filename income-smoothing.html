The Income Smoothing System takes your actual 12-month income, strips out the noise, and produces one reliable figure — your Smoothed Monthly Income. That single number becomes your salary, no matter what actually came in this month.

The infographic walks you through all four steps: entering your income data, categorising your expenses, understanding your three-way allocation, and building your four-account system.

The four-account system

One income. Four accounts. Zero guesswork.

Every rand you earn flows through a simple structure that keeps your living money, tax provision, and savings completely separated.

🌊
Account 1
The River

Your income hub. All client payments and invoices land here first. Nothing gets spent directly from The River.

🏠
Account 2
The Flow

Your living account. Receives your smoothed monthly income on the 1st of every month — the same amount, every month, regardless of what came in.

🏛️
Account 3
The Vault

Your tax provision. Every time a payment arrives, your tax percentage moves here immediately. Never spend money that belongs to SARS.

🛡️
Account 4
The Shield

Your buffer and savings. Absorbs the surplus in big months and covers the gap in lean ones. This is what makes a quiet month survivable.

Four steps

How the system works

The tool guides you through four screens, each building on the last. Takes about 15 minutes to complete.

1

Your Income

Enter what you actually earned in each of the past 12 months. A live bar chart updates as you type, flagging low-income and zero months. Include all sources: freelance, commission, side work, business drawings.

2

Your Expenses

Log every monthly expense and assign it to one of three tiers. Tier 1 is non-negotiable, Tier 2 is important, Tier 3 is flexible. The system uses your Tier 1 total to anchor your smoothed income.

3

Your System

See your personalised smoothed income, your three-way allocation (Living, Tax, Buffer), and a chart overlaying your actual income with the smooth line. This is your new financial foundation.

4

Your Plan

Receive your stability score, exact rand amounts for each of the four accounts, and a personalised 30-day action calendar to get the system running in real life.

The three-way split

Every rand has a job before you spend it.

Your smoothed monthly income is automatically divided into three allocations. Living covers your actual expenses. Tax prevents the SARS shock that catches most Lumpies off guard. Buffer builds The Shield month by month.

The formula is conservative by design — built from your lowest-earning months, not your average. That means it holds even in a genuinely bad quarter.

Lumpy tip: If you've completed this module your smoothed income is the figure that feeds directly into the Smart Spending Framework (Module 2) and the 5-Year Stability Plan (Module 3).
Your smoothed income allocation
Living — your actual expenses ~65%
Tax provision — into The Vault ~27%
Buffer — growing The Shield ~8%

Percentages are personalised to your actual income and expense data.
