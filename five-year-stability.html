Step 2 — The Shield

The foundation everything else sits on.

The emergency fund is the first and most important milestone. Before any retirement savings or investment, you need a liquid cash reserve that makes quiet months survivable without borrowing.

Your target is your Tier 1 monthly expenses multiplied by the number of months you choose. The Shield covers only the costs you cannot stop paying — rent, medical aid, loan repayments, insurance. Tier 2 and Tier 3 can wait.

When the target is reached, redirect the monthly contribution to your Retirement Annuity. Emergency fund first, then long-term savings.

Step 3 — Wealth building

Retirement savings tools that actually work in your favour.

As a self-employed Lumpy the SARS RA deduction is one of the most powerful tax incentives available to you. Contributing to your RA simultaneously saves for retirement and reduces your provisional tax bill.

Retirement Annuity

Tax-deductible retirement savings

SARS allows you to deduct up to 27.5% of your taxable income per year (capped at R430,000). Every rand you contribute reduces your taxable income — and therefore your provisional tax bill.

The Lumpy compound growth projection shows you exactly how much you'll have at retirement, and the impact of increasing contributions month by month.

27.5% max annual deduction
from taxable income
Tax-Free Savings Account

Completely tax-free growth

Contribute up to R36,000 per year (R3,000/month), with a lifetime limit of R500,000. All growth — interest, dividends, and capital gains — is completely free of tax.

Unlike the RA, your TFSA is more liquid and more flexible. Ideal for medium-term goals and retirement supplementation. Most Lumpy stability plans include both.
