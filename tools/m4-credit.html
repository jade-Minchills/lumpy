Step 1 of 5

Your credit profile

Tell us what you are applying for and where you stand. Lenders assess irregular income earners differently - this module shows you exactly what they look for and how to present your strongest case.

Lumpy tip: South African lenders use three credit bureaus - TransUnion, Experian, and Compuscan (XDS). You are entitled to one free credit report per year from each. A score of 610+ is the typical minimum for a home loan; 700+ qualifies for preferential rates.
Step 2 of 5

Proving your income to lenders

Without a payslip, you prove income through documentation. Lenders need at least 2 years of consistent, verifiable income history. Tell us what you have.

The tax minimisation paradox: If you have legitimately reduced your taxable income through SARS deductions, your ITA34 assessments will show a lower income figure than you actually earn. Banks use these assessments as income verification. Plan ahead - discuss with your tax practitioner how to balance tax efficiency against lender income requirements before applying for credit.

Your deposit & assets

A deposit reduces lender risk and significantly improves approval odds for Lumpies. Your asset base signals financial discipline and capacity to repay.

Your financial asset base

Months of repayments in reserve: South African banks respond positively to self-employed applicants who can demonstrate 3-6 months of the proposed repayment amount sitting in savings. This single factor can make the difference between approval and decline for Lumpies.
