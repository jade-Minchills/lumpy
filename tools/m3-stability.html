Tax-Free Savings Account

A Tax-Free Savings Account lets you save up to R36,000 per year (R3,000 per month), with a lifetime cap of R500,000. All growth - interest, dividends, and capital gains - is completely tax-free. Unlike a Retirement Annuity, you can access it at any time.

Step 4 of 5

Your protection layer

Salaried employees receive group risk cover automatically. As a Lumpy, you replace this yourself. These are not optional extras - they are the floor your plan sits on.

Medical aid structure

The protection gap for Lumpies: As a Lumpy working for yourself, employer sick pay and unemployment benefits do not apply to you. Your emergency fund (The Shield) and income protection policy are your personal safety net. This is why Step 2 - building the emergency fund - comes before Step 4.
